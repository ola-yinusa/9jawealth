Three things you own with every plot.

Permanent Land Ownership

Upon full payment you receive a Registered Survey and Deed of Assignment in your name or your company's name. The land is legally yours to hold, transfer, or pass down.

Structured Lifetime Income

Oil palm trees begin fruiting within 4 to 5 years, with projected first harvest payouts starting from Year 6 onward every year the plantation produces.

25–40+ Year Productive Asset

Oil palm is a long-cycle crop. A single plantation cycle can remain productive across a 25–40+ year tree lifespan, making this a genuine multi-generational asset.
