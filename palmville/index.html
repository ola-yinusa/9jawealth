What You're Actually Buying

Three things, permanently yours.

01

Permanent Land Ownership

Upon full payment you receive a Registered Survey and Deed of Assignment in your name or your company's name. The land is legally yours; to hold, transfer, or pass down.

02

Structured Lifetime Income

Once the plantation matures, net harvest proceeds are shared with you every year for as long as the plantation remains productive; without you lifting a hoe.

03

Up to 40 Years Productive Asset

Oil palm is a long-cycle crop. A single plantation cycle can remain productive for decades, which is what makes this a genuine multi-generational asset.
