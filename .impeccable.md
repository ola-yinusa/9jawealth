## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are Nigerians and Nigeria-adjacent audiences exploring wealth-building options through a financial self-assessment. They are likely cautious, trust-sensitive, and evaluating whether the brand is credible enough to engage with further around forex signals or real-estate investment opportunities.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, refined. The interface should evoke trust, composure, and financial seriousness without looking cold or corporate. It should avoid generic AI-fintech tropes, startup flashiness, loud gradients, and manipulative urgency.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light-first with restrained dark accents. Editorial finance rather than app-store fintech: warm paper-like backgrounds, navy structure, measured gold highlights, strong typography, and premium spacing. Motion should guide attention and reward interaction, but remain subtle, accessible, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

### Design Principles
1. Trust before excitement: every visual choice should increase credibility before conversion pressure.
2. Premium through restraint: use fewer, better accents rather than decorative excess.
3. Honest interfaces: the frontend should clearly communicate what is a live journey versus a preview or placeholder.
4. Interaction with purpose: animation should clarify state, hierarchy, and next steps.
5. Accessibility is part of polish: keyboard support, semantic structure, strong focus states, and readable contrast are baseline quality.
