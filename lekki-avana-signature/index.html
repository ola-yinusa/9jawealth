Title, Allocation & Terms Clarity

  • Included in 0–6 month prices: Land + Survey Plan + Legal Fee + Preliminary Development Fee.
  • Separate fee before building: A Main Infrastructure Fee applies before construction commences; the written Main Infrastructure Fee schedule is provided prior to commitment.
  • Title verification before deposit: The estate title and survey documents and current allocation details are shared with you in writing before any deposit.
  • Physical allocation vs. possession: Pay ₦5,000,000 to qualify for physical plot allocation; full legal possession of the land follows completion of full payment.
  • Provisional allotment & refund deduction policy: Allotments are provisional until the formal sales agreement is executed. Approved refunds are subject to a 35% administrative deduction payable within six months under the contract terms.
