Three checks worth making before you pay.

The right plot depends on what you can build, the documents behind it and the full cost of ownership. Use the developer’s published material as a starting point, then check the details for your specific plot.

Walk the site

Arrange an inspection, locate the plot on the estate layout and ask what type of building is allowed in its cluster.

Review the paperwork

Request the current title documents, survey, draft contract and allocation terms. Have an independent professional review them.

Confirm every charge

Get a written price schedule for your plot size, including deposit, instalments, infrastructure charges and the contract’s default and refund terms.
